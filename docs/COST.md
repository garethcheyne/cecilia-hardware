November 2026. Raspberry Pi prices have risen sharply through 2026 because
memory is scarce: check current prices before ordering. Prices for the
display, cables and printing are typical online prices, not quotes.

## Ways to bring it down

- **Pi 5 2 GB** saves about US$32. That suits a unit without touch screens;
  two screens running the console want 4 GB.
- **Kits:** sell the I/O board, display and case, and let the church buy the
  Pi locally. That cuts the price and stock risk, and it changes the
  certification paperwork (see [SPEC.md](SPEC.md#certification)).
- **Skip the display** on a basic model: about US$25 less, and the unit still
  shows its address on the screens at the console.
